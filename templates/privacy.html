Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

🔒 Short version: We do not store, sell, or share any text or documents you upload. Your data stays in your browser session.

1. What data we collect

We collect minimal information to operate and improve this service:

  • Usage analytics via Google Analytics (anonymized page views, session duration, feature usage). No personally identifiable information is collected.
  • No text or documents — any text you paste or files you upload are processed in memory only and are never written to disk or stored in a database.
  • Summary history is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage and never sent to our servers.

2. How we use data

  • To generate summaries and hallucination reports on demand.
  • To understand which features (models, file formats) are most used, so we can improve the app.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. Cookies & third-party services

  • Google Analytics uses cookies to track anonymous usage. You can opt out via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • No other third-party cookies or trackers are used.

4. Data retention

Uploaded files and pasted text are discarded immediately after summarization. No user content is retained on our servers at any time.

5. Your rights

Since we do not store personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions, contact us at privacy@yourapp.com.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top of the page will always reflect the most recent revision.
